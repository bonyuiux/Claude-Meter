# Claude Meter promo — product context

(Inferred from the build conversation on 2 Oct 2026; assumptions labelled.)

- **Product:** Claude Meter. A free, open-source macOS widget by Bon Yeung that floats on the desktop and shows how much
  of your Claude plan is left and when it resets. Clawd (the Claude Code character) races a fire of burned tokens to the
  reset flag. A pop-out bubble shows "Thinking…" and "Done!" for Claude Code tasks.
- **Surface:** a ~20 s silent promo video for a LinkedIn post. Portrait 4:5, 1080×1350, 30 fps, burned-in captions.
- **Mode:** Persuade. Viewers scroll a feed with the sound off, mostly on phones.
- **Audience (assumption):** designers, developers and AI-curious people who use Claude daily.
- **Voice:** warm, playful and honest. Captions are natural full sentences, never staccato fragments. No competitors named.
  It's an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Anthropic.
- **World (pinned):** "Loud Retro": ink #0B0A12, acid lime #E8FF00, violet #7C5CFF, cyan #22E0D6, pink #E0218A,
  Geist Pixel / Geist Mono / Geist, hard offset shadows, stepped motion. Pushed into an 80s arcade attract-mode cabinet.
